Frequently asked questions

Can I listen to my email while driving?

Yes. DriveMail Voice reads your unread email aloud on Apple CarPlay — the sender, subject, and body — so you can stay on top of your inbox without looking at your phone.

Can Siri read my emails to me?

Siri can read a notification aloud, but it can't open your inbox, read through unread messages in order, summarize, or reply for you. DriveMail Voice is a full hands-free email client for the car. More on Siri vs DriveMail →

Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?

Yes — Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud. Gmail uses an app password for a secure connection. How to use Gmail on CarPlay →

Is there another email app for CarPlay?

There's essentially one other, built for an older iOS. DriveMail Voice is the modern option with voice replies, AI summaries, and multi-account support. See the comparison →

Is my email private?

Yes. All processing happens on your device. Email content is held in memory only and never written to disk, and there are no backend servers — your data stays on your phone.

How much does it cost?

Free, with a small banner ad. A one-time $0.99 in-app purchase removes ads for good.
